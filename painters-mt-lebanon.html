Painting Services for Mt. Lebanon Properties

Mt. Lebanon projects can involve more than changing wall color. Interior rooms may include detailed trim, older patches, wallcoverings or connected spaces where the finish needs to remain consistent. Exterior projects require a clear surface-by-surface scope and preparation plan.

M.T. Christian Painting serves residential and commercial customers in Mt. Lebanon as part of its broader South Hills service area. The company provides painting along with drywall installation and repair, molding and trim, wallpaper services and power washing where those services fit the project.

Plan Around the Property You Actually Have

Before requesting an estimate, identify the rooms, elevations or architectural details that matter most. Note damaged drywall, peeling exterior coatings, worn trim, wallpaper or other conditions that may need attention before painting. This gives the conversation a concrete starting point.

For interiors, it is useful to decide whether the project should include only walls or also ceilings, doors, baseboards and casings. For exteriors, identify the main surfaces and separate trim or detail work. A defined scope makes it easier to compare priorities and understand the finished result.
