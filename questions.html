Frequently Asked Questions

What Customers Often Want to Know

These answers cover the most common questions about M.T. Christian Painting and the services available throughout the Pittsburgh area.

Does M.T. Christian Painting handle both residential and commercial painting?

Yes. M.T. Christian Painting works with homeowners, businesses, property managers and commercial properties throughout the Pittsburgh area.

Do you provide interior painting?

Yes. Interior painting is available for residential and commercial properties, including individual rooms, hallways, common areas, offices and larger interior projects.

Do you provide exterior painting?

Yes. Exterior painting is available for homes, businesses and other properties throughout the service area.

Can painting and repair work be handled on the same project?

Yes. Depending on the project, painting can be combined with drywall installation or repair, molding and trim work, wallpaper services and other related work.

Do you install and repair drywall?

Yes. M.T. Christian Painting provides drywall installation and drywall repair for residential and commercial projects.

Do you install or repair molding and trim?

Yes. Molding and trim installation and repair are available as part of residential and commercial projects.

Do you install wallpaper?

Yes. Wallpaper installation is available for residential and commercial interiors.

Do you remove wallpaper?

Yes. Existing wallpaper can be removed as part of a project.

Do you provide power washing?

Yes. Power washing is available for residential and commercial properties.

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Call 412-613-4177 to discuss your project and request a free estimate.

How long has M.T. Christian Painting been serving Pittsburgh-area customers?

M.T. Christian Painting has been serving Pittsburgh-area customers since 1992.

What areas does M.T. Christian Painting serve?

The service area includes Pittsburgh's South Hills, North Hills and surrounding communities, including Upper Saint Clair, Mount Lebanon, McMurray, Peters Township, Canonsburg, Bethel Park, Castle Shannon, Pleasant Hills, West Mifflin, Baldwin, Whitehall, Squirrel Hill, Carnegie, Kennedy, Robinson, Moon, Cranberry, Seven Fields, Hampton and Shaler.

Do you work with property managers?

Yes. M.T. Christian Painting works with property managers and can provide painting and related services for rental and commercial properties.

Do you work on offices and retail spaces?

Yes. Commercial work can include offices, professional spaces, retail locations, storefronts and other business properties.

Can you handle more than one type of work during the same project?

Yes. One advantage of working with M.T. Christian Painting is that several related needs can often be handled together, such as painting, drywall, trim and wallpaper work.

How do I get started?

Call 412-613-4177 and describe your residential or commercial project. M.T. Christian Painting can answer your questions and discuss the next step.
