Repair the Surface Before Finishing the Room

Paint does not remove a drywall defect. Holes, cracked areas, loose material, visible seams and poorly blended patches can remain noticeable—or become more noticeable—after a fresh finish is applied. The damaged area should be evaluated and repaired before the room is treated as a painting project.

M.T. Christian Painting provides drywall installation, finishing and repair for residential and commercial properties throughout Pittsburgh's South Hills and surrounding communities. Because interior painting is also available, the repair and final finish can be discussed as one coordinated scope.

Identify the Cause When Damage May Be Active

Some drywall damage is isolated and cosmetic. Other conditions may be connected to a leak, recurring moisture, movement or another building issue. Customers should correct an active source before the area is closed and refinished. A cosmetic repair is not a substitute for plumbing, roofing, structural or moisture remediation.

The estimate should identify the damaged area, the level of finishing expected and how much surrounding surface must be painted to produce a consistent appearance. A small patch does not always mean only the exact patch will need finish work.

Common Questions

Drywall Repair Questions

Can you paint after repairing the drywall?

Yes. Interior painting is available and can be included in the same project discussion. The estimate should define how much of the surrounding surface is included.

Do you provide drywall installation as well as repair?

Yes. The company's listed drywall services include installation, finishing and repair for residential and commercial properties.

Can you repair drywall damaged by a leak?

The visible drywall can be evaluated after the leak or moisture source has been properly corrected. Active water problems should be resolved before cosmetic finishing.

What information should I provide for an estimate?

Share the property location, whether the damage is on a wall or ceiling, approximate size, known cause, whether it is still active and whether painting should be included.
