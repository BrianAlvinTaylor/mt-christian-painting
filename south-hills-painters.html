Painting and Related Services Across the South Hills

Choosing a painter is about more than selecting a color. The quality of the finished room, exterior or commercial space depends on what happens before the finish coat: protecting nearby surfaces, correcting damaged areas, preparing the substrate and planning the work in the right order.

M.T. Christian Painting serves homeowners, businesses, property managers and investment properties throughout Pittsburgh's South Hills. The company has served Pittsburgh-area customers since 1992 and provides free estimates for projects ranging from a focused room update to larger residential and commercial work.

Start With the Actual Condition of the Property

Some projects are ready for paint after routine preparation. Others reveal cracked drywall, failed seams, damaged trim, old wallcoverings or exterior surfaces that need cleaning first. Because painting, drywall, finish carpentry, wallpaper and power washing are available through the same company, the estimate can account for related work instead of treating every issue as a separate project.
